📍 Kenya County Context — Modern Contraceptive Use Rate (KDHS 2022)
🟢 Embu
57.0%
Highest in Kenya. Strong central highland health infrastructure and high education levels.
🟢 Kirinyaga
54.2%
Second highest. Well-served county with strong community health promoter coverage.
⚪ National avg
37.9%
National average hides a 56-point spread. County-level targeting is essential for impact.
🔴 Garissa
4.5%
ASAL county. SHAP confirms the geographic barrier persists after controlling for wealth and religion.
🔴 Wajir
2.8%
North Eastern ASAL county. Clinic access and commodity supply chains are the primary barriers.
🔴 Mandera
1.2%
Lowest in Kenya. A 56-point gap below Embu — the signature finding of this project.
🏔
Geography is the biggest driver
County of residence outranks both wealth and education in predicting modern use. Cramér's V = 0.189 — the strongest predictor in the dataset.
💼
Employment is the most actionable lever
Employed women are significantly more likely to use modern methods. The 3rd strongest SHAP predictor — and the one most directly changeable through programmes.
💰
Wealth shows a cliff, not a gradient
Poorest → Poorer delivers a +16 percentage point jump in modern use. Above Poorer, three more quintiles add only 1.8pp combined.
👶
Contraception follows childbirth
has_given_birth is the #1 categorical predictor (Cramér's V = 0.368). Women overwhelmingly adopt contraception after having children, not before.
